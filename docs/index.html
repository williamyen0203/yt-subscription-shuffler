Privacy Policy

YouTube Subscription Shuffler — Effective date: August 5, 2026

YouTube Subscription Shuffler is a Chrome extension that plays a random video from your list of YouTube subscriptions. The developer does not operate any servers and does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. All data stays on your own device and is exchanged only with Google.

1. Data the extension accesses

  • Your YouTube subscriptions and videos — read from the YouTube Data API v3 using the youtube.readonly scope, to display your subscription list and pick a random video to play.
  • Your Google account email — read locally via chrome.identity to show which account you are signed in as.

2. Where data is stored

  • OAuth access token — stored in chrome.storage.session on your device. It is cleared when you sign out of the extension.
  • Excluded channels list — stored in chrome.storage.local on your device. It persists until you re-include the channels or uninstall the extension.

3. Data transmitted

  • Requests go directly from your browser to Google's YouTube Data API v3. There is no server owned by the developer.
  • The developer never receives, sells, shares, or uses your data for advertising or any other purpose.

4. Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions, which are used strictly for the features described above:

  • storage — store the excluded-channels list and the session access token.
  • identity / identity.email — sign in with your Google account.
  • Host access to *.googleapis.com — call the YouTube Data API.

5. Your control

  • Sign out of the extension to clear your access token.
  • Remove the extension to delete all data it stored on your device.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/williamyen0203/yt-subscription-shuffler.
