Never doomscroll your feed again.

Subscription Shuffler is a Chrome extension that plays a random video from your YouTube subscriptions — one click, straight into the video.

Get the extension

Features

Truly random

Picks a random subscription, then a random video from that channel's uploads — not just their latest upload.

Your data stays yours

Signs in with your own Google account. Subscriptions and exclusions live only on your device.

Exclude channels

Don't want a channel in the rotation? Exclude it with one click and see the full list anytime.

Lightweight

A simple popup that opens the video in a new tab. No background servers, no tracking.

How it works

After you sign in, the extension reads your subscription list with read-only access, shuffles it, picks a random video from the chosen channel, and opens it for you. Every shuffle is a fair roll — you might land anywhere in a channel's uploads.
